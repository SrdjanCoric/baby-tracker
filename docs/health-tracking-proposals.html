Health Tracking — 5 UI Proposals

Each proposal shows a dashboard card (how it appears on the home screen alongside feeding, sleep, diaper cards) and the logging screen (what the user sees when tapping + or the card).

Proposal 1 Unified Health Card

A single card that combines medications, temperature, and vaccinations into one view. The card always shows the most recent health event (last medication dose, last temperature reading) with a today badge counting total health logs. The logging screen uses tabs to switch between medication, temperature, and vaccination entry.

Best for: Parents who want one place for all health info without cluttering the dashboard with multiple cards.

Proposal 2 Medication Schedule Card

Prioritizes the medication schedule — shows what's due next and when the last dose was given. Designed for parents managing recurring medications (antibiotics, fever reducers). The card acts like a mini medication tracker with a countdown to the next dose.

Best for: Parents actively managing an illness where timing between doses is critical.

Proposal 3 Temperature-Forward Card

Leads with a prominent temperature reading — the number parents care about most when their baby is sick. The temperature is large and color-coded (green/amber/red). Medications and symptoms are shown as secondary info below. When there's no active fever being tracked, the card falls back to showing the last medication or a calm "All clear" state.

Best for: Fever monitoring scenarios where temperature is the #1 concern and parents need to see it at a glance.

Proposal 4 Split Sub-Sections Card

The card is divided into compact sub-sections showing the last event for each health type — temperature, medication, and vaccination — in a structured list. Each row has an icon, label, and the latest value/time. This gives a quick "health dashboard at a glance" without needing to tap in. The logging screen presents all three options as entry points.

Best for: Parents who want to see all health data points simultaneously without scrolling or tapping.

Proposal 5 Smart Status Card

An adaptive card that changes based on context. When the baby is healthy, it shows a calm "all clear" state with the last check-up info. When medications are active, it becomes a medication tracker. When a fever is detected, it shifts to fever monitoring mode with prominent temperature. The card intelligently surfaces what matters most right now.

Best for: Keeping the dashboard clean during healthy periods while surfacing critical info during illness.

Reference Proposal Comparison
Proposal Focus Complexity Tradeoff
1. Unified Everything in one card + tabs ⭐⭐ Simple but card can feel crowded
2. Med Schedule Medication timing & doses ⭐⭐⭐ Great for illness, less useful when healthy
3. Temp-Forward Temperature as hero metric ⭐⭐ Striking during fever, empty when healthy
4. Split Sections All data points visible at once ⭐⭐ Info-dense, but taller card on dashboard
5. Smart Status Adaptive to baby's current state ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Best UX but most logic to implement
