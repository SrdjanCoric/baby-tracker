Behaviour

Who sees it. Only the person who started the timer. Same rule as editing the start time. Everyone else sees today's screen.

Where. One grey text link, "✕ Discard timer", directly under the hint line ("Tap to stop" / "Tap to resume"), same size and colour as that hint. Present in both running and paused states. Same on sleep, feeding, pumping and tummy time.

Alert text. Title "Discard this sleep?" (feeding: "this feed", pumping: "this pumping session", tummy time: "this tummy time"). Body "3 min 41 s will not be saved. The timer is cleared on every phone." Buttons: "Keep timing" (cancel, bold) and "Discard" (destructive).

On Discard. Lock released, local timer cleared, Live Activity ended, nothing saved, sheet closes. Always asks, even under 60 seconds.

Not in scope

No Undo. No toast. No control on the Home card, the widget or the Watch.
