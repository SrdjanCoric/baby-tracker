# Nara Baby & Pregnancy Tracker

**Developer:** Nara Organics
**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Apple Watch
**App Store Rating:** 5 stars (Apple App Store & Google Play)
**Users:** 1 million+ parents
**Release Date:** Early 2019

---

## Executive Summary

Nara Baby is a highly-rated baby tracking app designed by a mom who found existing baby tracker apps "overly complex, UI-heavy, and a pain to use." The app is notably **free and ad-free** for core features, which is rare in the baby tracker market. While one third-party source mentions a $9.99/month advanced sleep scheduler option, official sources consistently emphasize the app is free with no upsells. Users consistently praise its clean interface, multi-caregiver syncing, and ease of use.

---

## Free Features

Nara Baby offers extensive features at no cost:

### Baby Tracking
- **Feeding tracking**: Breastfeeding timer (left/right side tracking with memory of last side), bottle feeding (formula or breastmilk) by time and amount, pumping timer for each side
- **Solids tracking**: Dozens of first foods preloaded, photo uploads and notes for feeding sessions
- **Diaper tracking**: Wet, dirty, or dry diapers, diaper rash logging
- **Sleep tracking**: Sleep timers for naps and nighttime, sleep pattern graphs

### Scheduling & Reminders
- **Recurring nap reminders** with two schedule types:
  - Fixed Times: Specific time for each reminder
  - Wake Windows: Adjustable reminders based on when child woke from last nap
- Sleep card displays "time until next nap" based on schedule

### Pregnancy Tracking
- Day-by-day pregnancy logging
- Vitals tracking: blood pressure, weight, temperature, blood glucose
- Health symptoms: nausea/morning sickness, fatigue, food cravings/aversions, headaches
- Doctor appointment tracking with notes
- Journal entries with photos

### Postpartum Recovery
- Hydration and food logging
- Sleep tracking for parents
- Daily mood tracking (happy to anxious scale)
- Journal entries
- Custom routines (yoga, exercise, snacktime)

### Multi-Caregiver & Device Features
- **Family sharing**: Add partners, grandparents, babysitters with full access
- **Real-time syncing** across multiple phones
- **Home screen widgets** for quick access to timers and status
- **Apple Watch support**

### Growth & Data
- Growth charts using WHO Child Growth Standards (CDC-recommended for children under 2)
- Percentiles for length/height-for-age, weight-for-age, head circumference
- History and trend functions (up to 14-day view in-app)
- Data export to spreadsheet (Excel) for custom analysis
- Milestone tracking with photos ("Firsts" feature)

## User Pros

### Design & Usability
- "Super simple, clean and beautiful interface" - consistently praised across reviews
- "Intuitive, calming design" - specifically designed to reduce stress for sleep-deprived parents
- "Aesthetically pleasing" and "most intuitive" compared to competitors
- One-handed operation friendly - "doesn't require a lot of clicking through screens"
- Can be as specific or simple as desired - unlike apps requiring all fields to be filled

### Multi-Caregiver Coordination
- Real-time syncing between partners, grandparents, babysitters
- "My husband and I both have the app and can track changes or feed independently"
- Anyone added sees updates instantly
- "Makes scheduling a breeze" and "easier for everyone to be on the same page"

### Ad-Free Experience
- "No clutter, articles, or ads" unlike competing apps
- "Other apps were trying to build an ecosystem" which interfered with usability
- Users ask "How can it be free? I don't fully understand!"

### Features
- Widgets for home screen - "convenient to just look at phone for quick reminder"
- Trends make doctor visits simpler
- Flexible - can remove tracking options no longer needed (e.g., breastfeeding when switching to formula)
- Works well for twin parents - "super easy to toggle back and forth"
- Timers can run simultaneously

### Customer Support
- "Wonderful experiences with tech support"
- Developers respond to issues and email users when fixes are released
- Active bug fixing and updates

### Value
- "Better than paid apps"
- Free version includes history/trends (often paywalled in competitors)

## User Cons

### Syncing Issues
- "If I start the sleep timer on my phone and my partner goes to stop it on his, it often keeps going on my phone"
- Watch logs sometimes don't appear on spouse's phone until iPhone app is opened
- No manual refresh option - must close and reopen app to see partner's updates
- "While it syncs quite well, there have been minor confusions when it didn't sync immediately"

### Technical Bugs
- Time selector sometimes "snaps back after being changed"
- Glitching and freezing reported (needs updates)
- Display issues - one user reported feeding times stopped showing after 6 months of use
- When changing minutes manually on sleep timer, changes start time instead of end time

### Missing Features
- **No native iPad app**
- Cannot add custom trackers (medicine, spit up, baths)
- Cannot create custom categories/tags for graphs
- **14-day trend limit** in-app (must export to Excel for longer analysis)
- Does not show total time or breakdown for left/right breastfeeding
- No display of how long baby has been awake on home screen
- Not strong for immunization tracking

### UI Annoyances
- OK/Cancel button placement for time entries is in a "terrible place" - accidental taps when scrolling
- Some users wish breastfeeding left/right buttons were accessible without tapping "+" first

### Limitations vs Competitors
- "Good for first few weeks but other apps might be better for sleep training when babies are older"
- Ranked #2 behind Talli by one comparison site
- Huckleberry may be better for sleep optimization with predictive features
