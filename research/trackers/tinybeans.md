# Tinybeans Private Family Album

## Overview
- **Developer:** Tinybeans
- **Price:** Free (Tinybeans+ for premium)
- **App Store Rating:** 4.9/5 (150,000+ ratings)
- **User Base:** 4+ million families in 200+ countries

## Pricing

| Plan | Cost |
|------|------|
| Monthly | $7.99/month |

| Free Trial | 14 days |

---

## Free Features

- 20 photos/videos per month upload limit
- 30-second maximum video length
- Single photo upload at a time
- Calendar-based organization
- Private sharing with invited family/friends
- Email notifications to viewers
- Contains advertisements

---

## Paid Features (Tinybeans+)

- Unlimited uploads
- 5-minute video length
- 2TB storage (~700K photos/videos)
- Ad-free experience
- Memory search functionality
- Hidden Moments (privacy feature)
- Photo editing, stickers, filters
- 300+ milestone tracking
- Photo collections and slideshows
- One-click backups
- Photo printing access

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros
- Excellent for grandparents - "Even my 85 year old grandfather uses it"
- Strong privacy (no social media exposure, 256-bit encryption)
- Email updates work great for non-tech-savvy family members
- Calendar organization praised
- "Perfect solution" for parents avoiding social media
- Good for international families to connect

### Cons
- Pricing frustration - long-time users felt "trapped" when free features were limited
- Technical issues - slow loading, caption deletion, video upload failures
- Poor customer service - unresolved issues for months
- 20 uploads/month too restrictive for active parents
- "Today" homepage bloated with blog posts instead of photos
- Some privacy skepticism despite company claims
