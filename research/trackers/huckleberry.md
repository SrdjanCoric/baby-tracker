- **User Base:** 5+ million families

## Free Features

- Sleep, feeding, diaper, growth, health, and activity tracking
- Breastfeeding timer with dual-side tracking
- Sleep summaries and history
- Multiple child profiles
- Multi-caregiver sync
- Reminders for medications and feedings

---

## Paid Features

### Plus ($9.99/mo or $58.99/yr)
- **SweetSpot** - AI sleep predictor (the most popular feature)
- Schedule Creator for age-appropriate sleep schedules
- Insights with data-driven tips (0-17 months)
- Enhanced reports and trend analysis
- AI Logging (text, voice, photo)
- Live Activities and Widgets (iOS)

### Premium ($14.99/mo or $119.99/yr)
- Everything in Plus
- **Berry** - 24/7 AI chat with expert guidance
- Custom sleep plans designed by sleep experts
- Weekly progress check-ins
- Ongoing expert support

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros

**SweetSpot Accuracy:**
- "It's a bit creepy, actually, how spot on it is"
- "My baby almost always falls asleep within a few minutes of his sweet spot"
- Predicts tiredness 10-15 minutes before nap time

**Sleep Success Stories:**
- Multiple users report babies sleeping through the night at 9-10 weeks
- "After 1 week, my baby started taking regular 90 min naps AND sleeping through the night"
- "Our lives did a 180 in the best possible way"

**General Praise:**
- "Saved my sanity - 100% worth the investment"
- "Couldn't be without it, takes all the guesswork out"
- Excellent for tracking milk protein allergy symptoms

### Cons

**SweetSpot Limitations:**
- "You can figure this out yourself by going with awake windows"
- "Made me obsess over naps even more and get frustrated"
- Wake windows become "way off" after 4-5 months for some

**Premium Value Concerns:**
- "Didn't seem like any new info we hadn't already tried"
- Not worth it if familiar with Babywise/Taking Cara Babies
- Custom plans "feel slightly generic"

**Technical Issues:**
- Data loss/entries disappearing
- Sync problems across devices
- Constant login issues with shared accounts

**Design Problems:**
- Recent updates feel "sloppy and rudimentary"
- Removed mixed diaper option
- No Android widgets
- Limited medication customization (5 max)

---

## Recommendation

**Best for:** First-time parents, 2-6 month age range, those unfamiliar with wake windows

**Skip if:** Already know sleep training methods, baby in daycare, tracking increases your anxiety

**Strategy:** Use the 14-day free trial. If SweetSpot helps, get annual Plus ($4.92/mo). Only upgrade to Premium during sleep regressions.
