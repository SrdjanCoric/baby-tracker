# Baby Tracker! by Amila Tech Limited

**App Store:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/baby-tracker/id1444238371)
**Google Play:** [Android Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amila.parenting)
**Developer Website:** [Amila.io](https://amila.io/)

**Rating:** 4.9 stars (129K+ reviews on Google Play, 1M+ downloads)

---

## Executive Summary

Baby Tracker by Amila Tech Limited is a highly-rated, comprehensive baby tracking app that excels at individual use with its intuitive interface and robust free features. However, its multi-user/caregiver syncing capabilities are a significant weakness that frustrated many users who share childcare duties.

---

## Free Features

The free version (ad-supported) includes:

### Core Tracking
- **Feeding Tracking**
  - Breastfeeding (with timer and side tracking)
  - Bottle feeding (formula or expressed milk)
  - Solid foods
- **Sleep Tracking**
  - Nap timing with duration tracking
  - Timer continues running even when app is closed
- **Diaper Changes**
  - Pee, poop, or mixed
- **Pumping**
  - Breast pumping sessions with timer
- **Growth Tracking**
  - Height/length
  - Weight
  - Head circumference
  - WHO growth standards comparison

### Data Features
- Daily, weekly, and monthly activity analysis
- Charts and graphs for pattern identification
- Export reports as PDF
- Export raw data as CSV
- Calendar view
- Notes functionality

### Platform Support
- iOS (requires iOS 18.0+)
- Android
- Wear OS (Android smartwatch support)
- Mac (requires macOS 15.0+ with Apple M1 chip or later)
- Apple Vision (visionOS 2.0+)

### Language Support
34+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and many more.

## User Pros (Positive Feedback)

### Ease of Use
- "This is so simple and great for tracking everything for baby!" - Multiple users praise the intuitive, clean interface
- Designed for quick one-handed operation while holding baby
- Users report downloading it on delivery day and using it seamlessly for months
- Timer keeps running even when app is closed - helpful when you forget to stop it

### Pattern Recognition
- "I couldn't do pumping, breastfeeding, diapering, nap time and feeding times without it. My baby is honestly happier and less fussy because I know what she needs when it's time to nap, eat or change diaper."
- Charts and graphs help identify baby's patterns and behaviors
- Daily/weekly/monthly analysis helps parents plan better

### Comprehensive Tracking
- Tracks feeding, sleep, diapers, pictures, measurements, medicine, and more in one place
- Easy to see which side you last breastfed on and for how long
- Napping feature is praised - timer runs reliably without timing out

### Data Flexibility
- Can go back and edit entries after the fact
- PDF and CSV export options
- Ability to add notes to entries

### Developer Support
- Developers actively respond to user feedback via email
- Regular updates addressing user-reported issues
- High satisfaction rating (98% according to systematic review)

### Reliability
- One study found it had "the highest accuracy and precision" compared to other baby tracking apps
- 4.9 star rating maintained across platforms

## User Cons (Negative Feedback)

### Multi-User/Caregiver Syncing - MAJOR ISSUE
This is the most frequently cited complaint:

- "Great app, but only if you're the only one doing 100% of the care for your little one"
- "Would be perfect if there were some way to use on other devices"
- "Terrible for syncing data with a partner or spouse, or other caregiver"
- Users report having to text feeding/diaper information to each other to log later
- Data synchronization issues between devices - one person's entries can override another's
- No option to add another caregiver to the same account natively
- Some users created third-party tools to merge data files (exported as .abt files which are zipped JSON)

### Data Transfer/Backup Issues
- "If you change devices you can't transfer over the previous data"
- When phone breaks, there's no way to recover data if not backed up
- Import/export file method is the only option, which doesn't help with broken devices

### Interface Quirks
- Time entry interface complaint: "After an update the app reverted to an annoying clock that you had to physically move when adding times"
- Selecting specific times can be frustrating with the clock dial interface
- Graphs can be "hard to read visually" due to zoom function limitations
- Some users requested an undo feature for accidental deletions

### Ads (Free Version)
- Contains ads, though most users report they are "not too many" and "discreet"
- Ads appear at top and bottom of screen
- Premium required to remove all ads

### Missing Features (User Requests)
- No undo button for accidental deletions
- Calendar event recording was historically difficult (reportedly fixed in 2023 update)
- Limited multi-child profile management for some users

## Overall Assessment

### Confidence Level: High
Multiple sources (AppGrooves 99K+ reviews, App Store reviews, Google Play reviews, systematic research studies) consistently agree on both strengths and weaknesses.

### Best For:
- Single caregiver tracking
- Parents who primarily care for baby themselves
- Users who value simplicity and intuitive design
- Budget-conscious parents (robust free version)

### Not Recommended For:
- Households with multiple caregivers
- Parents who share duties equally with partner
- Situations involving nannies, grandparents, or sitters who also track

### Key Takeaway:
Baby Tracker by Amila is an excellent, highly-rated app for individual use with one of the most comprehensive free offerings in the category. However, the lack of proper multi-user synchronization is a dealbreaker for many modern parenting situations where childcare duties are shared.
