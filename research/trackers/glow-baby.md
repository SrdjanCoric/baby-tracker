# Glow Baby Tracker & Growth App

**Developer:** Glow Inc.
**Platform:** iOS and Android
**App Store Rating:** 4.7/5 (based on 22,484+ reviews)
**Last Updated:** January 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Glow Baby is a comprehensive AI-powered baby tracking app that offers robust free tracking features but locks comparative insights, AI predictions, and multi-caregiver access behind a premium subscription (~$60/year individual, ~$90/year family). Users praise its intuitive interface and comprehensive tracking, but criticize aggressive ads in the free version and significant privacy concerns documented by Mozilla and Consumer Reports.

---

## Free Features

The free version of Glow Baby includes:

### Core Tracking
- **Feeding Tracking**: Breastfeeding sessions (duration, side), bottle feeding (amount), and solid foods
- **Diaper Changes**: Log wet and dirty diapers with timestamps
- **Sleep Tracking**: Record sleep sessions and nap times
- **Pumping Sessions**: Track milk production and pumping frequency
- **Physical Growth**: Log height, weight, and head circumference
- **Milestones**: Track developmental milestones at 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18 months, and 2, 3, 4, 5 years
- **Health Tracking**: Temperature, symptoms, medications (limited to 5 custom medications)
- **Daily Activities**: Tummy time, play time, baths, etc.
- **Unlimited Children**: Track multiple children on one account

### Other Free Features
- Basic data visualization and graphs
- Daily tips and advice pop-ups
- Access to community forums
- Basic articles and resources

### Free Version Limitations
- **No caregiver sharing**: Cannot sync with partner or other caregivers
- **No comparative insights**: Cannot compare baby's metrics to other Glow babies
- **No AI predictions**: Cannot access AI-powered sleep/feeding predictions
- **Frequent ads**: Pop-up advertisements throughout the app
- **Limited customization**: Cannot edit standard options (medications, symptoms)
- **No premium articles**: Limited access to educational content

## Premium Features (Glow Premium)

Upgrading to Glow Premium unlocks:

### AI-Powered Features
- **AI Predictions**: Get forecasts for baby's next nap and feeding times
- **Personalized Insights**: AI learns from your input to provide tailored recommendations
- **Smart Scheduling**: Predictions improve as you log more data

### Comparative Insights
- Compare your baby's growth metrics to millions of other Glow babies
- See how feeding patterns, sleep times, and development compare to averages
- "Is this normal?" feature for peace of mind

### Multi-Caregiver Access (Family Plan)
- Add unlimited caregivers (partner, grandparents, babysitters)
- Sync data across all caregivers' devices
- Real-time updates when anyone logs information

### Additional Premium Benefits
- **Ad-free experience**: Remove all pop-up advertisements
- **Premium Articles**: Exclusive access to in-depth content from top-rated sources
- **Charts and Analytics**: Advanced data visualization
- **Guided Meditations**: Relaxation content for parents
- **Profile Customization**: Personalize your Glow Community profile
- **PDF Export**: Download all child data as a PDF report
- **Private Messaging**: Connect with other parents in the community

## User Cons

### Free Version Issues
- "Inundated with pop-up ads" - ads appear frequently, even when holding crying baby
- "The new format is very busy" - harder to navigate and log information
- "So frustrating it's almost not worth it" - some users find free version unusable
- Must pay to compare baby's metrics to other babies
- Cannot log medications or conditions like diaper rash without premium

### Syncing Problems
- "What one parent logs won't show up on the other parent's app"
- Manual server sync "only sometimes resolves the problem"
- Entries sometimes appear twice, and deleting one removes both
- Premium subscription not shared between devices/users

### Technical Issues
- After updates, app becomes "extremely laggy"
- "Clicking to add/start a log takes a long time to load"
- Some users report crashes and skipped information
- "They're pushing AI I don't want" - some find AI features intrusive

### Customization Limitations
- "You can't edit anything once you've clicked saved"
- "No place for doctors visits or notes"
- Cannot add notes to diaper or feeding entries
- "Most standard options (medications, symptoms) you can't edit or customize"
- Limited to 5 custom medications, requiring frequent re-entry
- Cannot remove unused items from main view (e.g., breastfeeding after switching to bottles)

### Feature Bloat
- "They keep adding silly features that get in the way"
- AR photo book described as "gimmicky"
- Constant forum notification spam
- Stories feature adds "clutter and nonsense"

### Pricing Complaints
- "Its just too basic for my taste especially for the price"
- Frequent pushing of premium version described as "annoying"
- Each caregiver expected to pay separately for premium
- No potty training features (some switch to Huckleberry for this)

### Privacy Concerns (Significant)
- Mozilla's "Privacy Not Included" gives Glow a poor rating
- 2020 California AG settlement ($250,000 fine) for privacy violations
- Consumer Reports found security vulnerabilities in 2016 and 2020
- Collects extensive personal data including location, sexual activity, mood
- Data can be used for targeted advertising
- May share data with "professional advisors" including lawyers and insurers
- Data not protected by HIPAA - can legally be sold to marketers
- Mozilla recommends users "look elsewhere for a privacy protecting" app

## AI Predictions Feature - User Experiences

### How It Works
- AI predicts baby's sleeping and feeding patterns based on logged data
- Predictions improve as more data is entered
- Premium-only feature

### Positive Feedback
- "Glow Baby stands out for its AI prediction feature, which you can use to get a handle on your baby's sleeping and feeding schedule"
- App "learns from your input, offering guidance and support based on your specific parenting journey"

### Criticisms
- "They're pushing AI I don't want" - some find it intrusive
- AI features behind paywall frustrates free users

- UI for AI features described as "form over function"

## Bottom Line

**Best For:** First-time parents who want comprehensive tracking, community support, and don't mind paying for premium features. Works well for single-caregiver households or those willing to pay for the Family Plan.

**Not Ideal For:** Privacy-conscious users, those wanting free multi-caregiver sync, parents needing potty training features, or users frustrated by aggressive upselling.

**Alternatives to Consider:** Huckleberry (better sleep predictions, potty training), Baby Tracker (simpler, free), or Sprout (different feature set).
