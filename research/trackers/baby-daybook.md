## Free Features

The free version includes:
- Basic activity tracking (feeding, sleep, diapers)
- Timer functionality
- Limited statistics and timeline access
- PDF export (recent activity only)
- Basic widgets and Apple Watch access
- **7-day free trial** of all premium features

**Important:** Free version has intrusive full-screen ads between tools.

---

## Paid Features (Premium)

- **Family Sync:** Real-time sharing with partners, grandparents, nanny - all connected caregivers get Premium at no extra cost
- **Sleep Predictions:** Personalized sleep schedule with predicted naps/bedtime, smart reminders
- **Advanced Statistics:** Full charts, unlimited history, custom date ranges
- **Growth Charts:** WHO/CDC percentiles, special Down Syndrome charts (unique feature)
- **Ad-free experience**
- **Full Apple Watch complications**
- **Lock Screen widgets and Siri Shortcuts**
- **Multiple baby profiles**

## Apple Watch Analysis

Generally praised - "syncs perfectly with my phone." Features include:
- Starting/stopping activities
- Viewing reminders
- Multiple complication sizes
- Multi-baby support on watch face
- Requires watchOS 9.0+

Some users want improvements to complications.
