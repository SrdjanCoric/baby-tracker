# Sprout Baby Tracker - Comprehensive Research

**Developer:** Med ART Studios
**Platform:** iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, visionOS (Android also available)
**App Store Rating:** 4.8 stars (average)
**Recognition:** Named "Best Baby Tracker App" by Consumer Reports and Forbes Health; Featured in Apple's WWDC 2024 Keynote

---

## Executive Summary

Sprout Baby Tracker is a well-established, feature-rich baby tracking app that has been helping families since 2010. It offers comprehensive tracking for feeding (including tube feeding), sleep, diapers, growth, and milestones. The app is known for its Apple Watch integration with hands-free double-tap gesture support and real-time family syncing. While highly rated overall, recent UI updates have drawn criticism from some long-time users, and syncing issues have been reported.

## Paid Features (Premium Subscription)

### Feeding Tracking
- **Breastfeeding tracker** - Times, records, and charts each nursing session
- **Bottle feeding tracker** - Tracks breast milk or formula feeding sessions
- **Solid food tracker** - Logs solid foods and baby's reactions
- **Tube feeding tracker** - Comprehensive tracking for tube-fed babies (developed in collaboration with Growing Independent Eaters)
- **Pumping tracker** - Records and charts pumping sessions

### Sleep & Diaper Tracking
- Sleep tracker with timers for each sleep session
- Diaper change logging (pee, poo, or both)
- Bowel movement color and texture tracking

### Growth & Health
- Growth tracker with height, weight, and head circumference
- WHO and CDC percentile comparisons and growth curves
- Developmental milestones tracker using CDC guidelines
- Health tracker for symptoms, temperature, medications, and illnesses
- Mood tracker to correlate feeding/sleep with behavior

### Smart Features
- **Apple Watch app** - Log feeding, sleep, and diaper changes on the go
- **Double-tap gesture** (watchOS 11) - Start/stop timers hands-free while holding baby
- **Live Activities** - See feeding and sleep progress on Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch
- **Interactive widgets** - Quick access and timer controls from home screen
- **Siri Shortcuts** - Voice commands for logging activities hands-free

### Family & Sharing
- Family sharing with real-time sync across devices
- Invite family members and caregivers to collaborate
- Multiple child profiles

### Data & Reports
- Visual summaries and trend analysis
- Detailed reports for feeding, sleep, and diaper patterns
- Export capabilities for doctor visits
- Photo journal for milestones
- Export memories into keepsake book

### Tube Feeding Features (Specific)
- Detailed tube feeding session records
- Celebratory milestone tracking ("firsts")
- Weekly journal for emotional and physical progress
- Charts to visualize the weaning journey
- Developed in collaboration with Growing Independent Eaters (GIE)

## User Pros (Positive Feedback)

### Ease of Use
- "Very easy to track baby's food, sleep, diapers and more"
- "I really like the daily summaries for each task so I can quickly see how much my kid has eaten or slept"
- "The interface is very easy and rather pleasing to use"
- One-handed logging with redesigned UI (buttons at bottom of screen)

### Multi-Device & Family Syncing
- "Multiple family members can download it and have it all synced up"
- "I know when a nap is active or how many oz he consumed recently if I'm out and about while my partner is taking care of our baby at home"
- Real-time sync across multiple devices
- "The best part is that my husband also uses the app. Only 1 person has to purchase it"

### Comprehensive Features
- Set reminders for feeding, diapers, and naps
- Track medications when baby is sick
- Growth curves with percentiles for pediatric visits
- Can add pictures to symptom tracker (helpful for allergic reactions)
- Medical records feature highly praised for doctor visits

### Long-term Reliability
- "I've used this app for both my children and can't survive without it"
- Users report 5-7+ years of continuous use across multiple children
- "Always been really reliable" and "worth every cent"
- Used first child's data as reference for second child

### Apple Watch Features
- Hands-free tracking with double-tap gesture (watchOS 11)
- "Using the app on Apple Watch in a pinch" is convenient
- Live Activities show progress on Watch face

### Responsive Development
- "Developers are super responsive"
- User reported requesting a feature via survey and it appeared two weeks later

### Privacy
- No accounts required
- No data selling
- Data kept private and secure

### Tube Feeding Support
- Recognized as valuable tool for special needs families
- Collaboration with feeding specialists at GIE
- Comprehensive tube feeding and weaning journey tracking

## User Cons (Negative Feedback)

### Recent Update Issues
- "Update is filled with bugs. UI changes were unnecessary and make the app less user friendly"
- "Everything now requires more steps to go through and it's just not convenient to use"
- Performance reported as slow after updates
- Long-time users canceling renewals due to UI changes

### Syncing Problems
- "Worked with support for a week trying to fix syncing issues and it still wouldn't work"
- "The sleep totals don't seem to always transfer from the nighttime hours"
- "The sleep timer doesn't always sync between my phone and my wife's"
- Setup process described as "not easy or pedagogical" even for tech-savvy users

### Timer & Live Activities Bugs
- Active feedings that have been ended still show as ticking on Home/Lock Screen
- "Occasionally getting stuck in a nursing session that won't stop"
- Have to force close app and manual refresh to see spouse's data

### Data Entry Issues
- After updates, app doesn't track ounces immediately
- Pumping can no longer separate between left and right side amounts
- Saves as 0oz requiring manual edit to add amount
- "Won't even record the amounts of a bottle, just the time and session length"

### Apple Watch Limitations
- Some users wish Apple Watch functionality was "more robust"
- Watch complications/syncing not always reliable

### Growth Tracker Criticism
- "I hate their growth tracker, specifically the weight tracking"
- Graphs difficult to read in dark mode (red barely visible)

### Milestone Anxiety
- App milestones can create pressure: "Every time the app said their baby should be able to do something by a certain week and they couldn't, it started to make me feel like something was wrong"

### Privacy Concerns
- Mozilla researchers unable to find privacy policy for related Sprout Pregnancy app
- Developer did not respond to Mozilla's privacy inquiries

## Recommendations

### Consider Sprout Baby Tracker If:
- You need comprehensive all-in-one tracking (feeding, sleep, diapers, growth, health)
- You have a tube-fed baby or child with special feeding needs
- You want Apple Watch integration with hands-free tracking
- Multiple family members need to track and sync data
- You plan to use the app for multiple children over several years
- Medical records and doctor visit preparation is important to you

### Consider Alternatives If:
- Sleep tracking and optimization is your primary concern (consider Huckleberry)
- You want a completely free option with no subscription
- You've experienced syncing issues with other apps and need bulletproof sync
- You prefer a simpler interface with fewer features

### Trial Recommendation
Take advantage of the 1-week free trial to test all Premium features before committing to a subscription. Pay particular attention to:
- Apple Watch functionality if that's important to you
- Syncing between devices with your partner
- The specific tracking features you need most
