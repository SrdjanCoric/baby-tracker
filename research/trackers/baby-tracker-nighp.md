## Free Features

The free version includes **all core tracking functionality**:

- **Feeding tracking:** Breastfeeding (with timer, left/right breast), bottle feeding, solid foods
- **Diaper changes:** Wet, dirty, mixed with notes
- **Sleep tracking:** Nap and nighttime sleep with duration
- **Pumping sessions:** Breast pump tracking with timer
- **Growth tracking:** Height, weight, head circumference
- **Health logging:** Medications, vaccines, temperature, doctor visits
- **Milestones:** Custom categories for baby's "firsts"
- **Activities:** Bath time, tummy time, outdoor time
- **Photo gallery:** Attach photos to entries
- **Multi-device sync:** Via Baby Tracker Server
- **iCloud/Dropbox backup:** Cloud backup options
- **Data export:** HTML format for doctors

**Free Version Limitations:**
- Contains advertisements
- No Apple Watch functionality
- No Siri Shortcuts

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros
- **Simplicity:** "Exactly what we needed with none of the overwhelming bulk that other apps have"
- **Multi-device sync:** "My family and I can log from either my phone, spouse phone or our iPad"
- **Medical value:** "I can literally show his doctor how often he eats, diapers, and sleep"
- **Privacy:** Developer does not collect any user data
- **Value:** One-time $5 payment, no subscription
- **Professional endorsement:** "Recommended by a pediatrician who uses it for their own child"

### Cons
- **Apple Watch issues:** "The watch app does not sync to my phone well and always says there is an error"
- **Sync delays:** "Updates from one partner don't show on the other's app until hours later"
- **Widget limitations:** "Wish I could record nursing sessions right on the widget"
- **Export format:** Only HTML export, users want CSV/Excel
- **Data transfer:** Data doesn't auto-transfer from free to Pro version
- **Dark mode readability:** "Not enough contrast between text and background"
