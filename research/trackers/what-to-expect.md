# What to Expect (Pregnancy Baby Tracker)

## Overview
- **Developer:** Everyday Health
- **Price:** Free
- **App Store Rating:** 4.9/5 (300,000+ ratings)
- **Notable:** Called the pregnancy "bible"; trusted by 15+ million parents; featured in Women's Health; doctor recommended

**Note:** What to Expect operates primarily as a free, ad-supported app. Unlike some competitors, it doesn't prominently advertise premium subscription tiers.

---

## Free Features

### Pregnancy Tracking
- Week-by-week pregnancy tracker with detailed baby development information
- Due date calculator and visual countdown
- Themed baby size comparisons (fruits or familiar objects)
- 3D videos showing baby development in utero
- Symptom tracking for morning sickness, nausea, etc.
- Daily personalized tips and updates
- Thousands of medically accurate, expert-reviewed articles

### Baby Tracking (Post-Birth)
- Feeding tracker (breastfeeding sessions)
- Bottle tracking for formula feeding
- Pump session logging
- Diaper change tracking
- Tummy time tracking
- Month-by-month milestone tracker (newborn to toddler)
- Home screen widgets for quick access

### Community Features
- Active community forums (new post every few seconds)
- Birth month groups
- Topic-specific groups (fertility, relationships, anxiety, etc.)

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros

**Content Quality:**
- "One of the longest known, most trusted pregnancy resources"
- Information reviewed by panel of experts
- Called the pregnancy "bible"
- Weekly videos helpful for understanding development
- "Insanely accurate" tracking

**Community:**
- "Huge community of people" with shared experiences
- Very active forums
- Birth month groups create sense of belonging
- "Found the community extremely helpful"

**Features & Value:**
- Completely free with comprehensive functionality
- Baby size comparisons are fun and engaging
- Feeding tracker is a "saving grace" for breastfeeding
- "Most other apps are weird, don't track properly, or you have to pay"
- Sends free samples, coupons, gift cards
- Nearly perfect 5-star rating

### Cons

**Privacy Concerns (MAJOR ISSUE):**

Mozilla "Privacy Not Included" Review Findings:
- Collects, uses, shares, and **sells** extensive personal information
- Data collected: name, precise location, due date, gender, age, nationality, job title, education, address, phone, email, social media, photos, browsing history
- Shares data with third-party advertisers
- Can disclose to "legal and regulatory authorities upon request"
- Security researcher found "full account takeover" vulnerability

Consumer Reports found the app "mostly disappointing as far as privacy and security"

**Advertising:**
- "Considerable number of ads" in the interface
- Some users find ads intrusive
- 10% of negative reviews mention ads

**Technical Issues:**
- App crashes and glitches reported
- Breastfeeding timer crashes when running in background
- Update-related bugs

**Content Limitations:**
- Assumes every pregnancy will end in live birth
- Difficult to share progress with friends/family
- Lack of representation and cultural sensitivity noted

**Community Concerns:**
- Forum posts can make users nervous/anxious
- "Scary" posts can be triggering
