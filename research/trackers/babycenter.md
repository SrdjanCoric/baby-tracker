# BabyCenter Pregnancy & Baby Tracker

## Overview
- **Developer:** BabyCenter LLC (owned by Everyday Health / Ziff Davis)
- **Price:** Free with in-app purchases
- **App Store Rating:** 4.89/5 (270,000 ratings)
- **User Base:** 400+ million parents globally
- **Notable:** 10+ million downloads; Apple featured app; Birth Club community; available since 2010

---

## Pricing

| Tier | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Free | $0 | Full access to core features, ad-supported |
| Premium | Unknown | Not prominently offered |

**Note:** BabyCenter is 99% funded by advertising revenue. Unlike competitors, it does not prominently advertise a premium subscription tier.

---

## Free Features

### Pregnancy Tracking
- Daily pregnancy updates and week-by-week development information
- 3D fetal development videos and visualizations
- Baby size comparisons (fruits/foods each week)
- Estimated baby length and weight at each stage
- Due date calculator and countdown
- Pregnancy symptoms tracker

### Health & Wellness
- Tips about common pregnancy symptoms
- Pregnancy workouts and nutrition advice
- All content medically reviewed by BabyCenter Medical Advisory Board

### Planning Tools
- Pregnancy calendar for appointments
- Baby Names Finder
- Baby Registry Checklist
- Hospital bag checklist (printable)
- Birth plan worksheet (exportable to PDF)
- Contraction timer
- Baby kick counter/tracker

### Educational Content
- Online birth class (free)
- Baby safety classes (free)
- Weekly articles and slideshows
- Award-winning videos

### Photo & Memory Features
- "Bumpie" photo tool for weekly pregnancy selfies
- Photo journal for baby's first year

### Community Features
- Birth Clubs (join parents with babies due the same month)
- Community forums and discussion boards

### Post-Birth Features
- Baby growth tracker
- Baby sleep log
- Feeding schedules and guides
- Tracks through toddler years
- Multiple children support

## Paid Features

BabyCenter's premium tier is not well-documented. The app relies primarily on advertising rather than subscriptions.

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros

**Content Quality:**
- "They really did their research" - users praise depth of information
- Medically reviewed by advisory board
- Comprehensive coverage from conception through toddler years

**Ease of Use:**
- "Completely free, easy to use"
- Clean interface showing days/weeks remaining
- Can track multiple pregnancies and children

**Educational Value:**
- "Every symptom, every cramp - this app had it all"
- Free birth and baby safety classes
- Birth plan creator exports to PDF

**Community (Positive):**
- Very active Birth Clubs
- "I wouldn't have survived the weeks after my miscarriage without the community boards"

**Value:**
- "The best part, completely free!"
- Helpful for registry building

### Cons

**Toxic Community/Forums (MAJOR ISSUE):**
- Sitejabber rating: 1.8/5 stars (community complaints)
- "The community is a cesspool of hateful, mentally unstable women"
- Reports of harassment and cyberbullying
- "Mean girl mob mentality"
- Moderators don't address issues
- **Recommendation:** "You'll find better communities on Reddit"

**Privacy & Data Concerns (SERIOUS):**
- Mozilla "Privacy Not Included" warns about privacy red flags
- Collects extensive personal data (due dates, photos, health info)
- Data is used, shared, and **sold** for advertising
- Named in US Senate Commerce Committee investigation
- Privacy policy over 10,000 words long

**Advertising:**
- Intrusive ads throughout the app
- Brand-embedded advertising within content
- No clear ad-free option

**Technical Problems:**
- Buggy photo uploads
- No edit button for mistyped information
- App crashes reported

---

## Privacy Warning

BabyCenter collects and sells extensive personal data including:
- Name, precise location, due date, gender, age
- Nationality, job title, education, work experience
- Address, phone, email, social media, photos
- Browsing history

Data is shared with third-party advertisers.

---

## Recommendation

**Use it if:** You want free, comprehensive pregnancy information and don't mind ads

**Avoid if:** Privacy is a concern, you want supportive community (try Reddit instead), or you need ad-free experience
