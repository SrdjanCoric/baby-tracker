# Ovia Parenting & Baby Tracker

**Developer:** Ovia Health (Labcorp)
**Platform:** iOS and Android
**App Store Rating:** 4.56/5 (based on ~13,000 ratings)
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2025

---

## Executive Summary

Ovia Parenting & Baby Tracker is a free-to-download baby tracking app that offers comprehensive daily logging features, 1000+ expert articles, and community support. While the core app is free, premium features (Ovia+) are typically accessed through employer or health plan benefits rather than direct consumer purchase. Users praise its ease of use and expert content but criticize glitchy feeding trackers, missing analytics features, and significant privacy concerns around data sharing with employers.

---

## Free Features

### Core Tracking
- **Feeding Tracker:** Log breastfeeding sessions (with side tracking), bottle feeds (ounces, type - formula or breastmilk), and timing
- **Breastfeeding Timer:** Time sessions as you go or log manually; can exit app and return to timer
- **Diaper Tracker:** Log wet and dirty diapers (#1s and #2s)
- **Sleep Tracker:** Monitor baby's sleep patterns
- **Milestone Checklists:** Track developmental milestones from postpartum through first year and beyond

### Content & Education
- **1,000+ Expert Articles:** Topics include breastfeeding, infant care, sleep training, child development, and parenting tips
- **Daily Personalized Updates:** Articles and tips synced with your child's age and development stage
- **Clinical Review:** All content reviewed by Ovia's in-house clinical team

### Family & Sharing
- **Multi-Child Support:** Add multiple children with personalized updates for each
- **Caregiver Sharing:** Share tracking info with partner and other caregivers
- **Family Calendar:** View all saved memories in one place
- **Photo & Video Sharing:** Share family moments with invited friends and family
- **Customization:** Customize each child's name, gender, and skin tone

### Community
- **Anonymous Community:** Ask and answer questions anonymously with other parents
- **Support Network:** Connect with community of parents and caregivers

## Paid Features (Ovia+)

Ovia+ is the premium tier that includes additional features beyond the free app:

### Health Coaching & Support
- **Ovia Care Team Access:** Connect with experts 7-days-a-week with unlimited sessions
- **Expert Specialties:** Nutrition, lactation support, doula and midwifery support, infant sleep coaching
- **Year-Round Availability:** Care Team available 365 days a year

### Clinical Programs
- **50+ Clinical Programs:** Step-by-step guidance and evidence-based programs designed by clinicians
- **Specialized Programs:** Support for birth control tracking, endometriosis, PCOS, and more
- **Personalized Content:** Tailored to your unique journey and goals

### Additional Benefits
- **Health Plan Navigation:** Help finding breast pumps, understanding fertility coverage, navigating pregnancy leave
- **Personalized Experience:** Based on your specific health plan benefits

**Note:** Ovia does not publicly list individual subscription prices. The premium version is primarily distributed through employer/health plan partnerships rather than direct consumer purchase. If Ovia+ is not available through your employer or health plan, you retain access to all free app features.

## User Pros (Real User Feedback)

### Ease of Use
- "So easy to use and so informative" - praised for intuitive interface
- Easy transition from Ovia Pregnancy app with familiar dashboard layout
- Can exit app during feeding and return to timer when ready
- One-handed use possible during nursing sessions

### Content Quality
- Expert-backed articles praised for going "into great detail week by week"
- Over 1,000 articles covering comprehensive parenting topics
- Content personalized based on baby's age

### Tracking Features
- "The various trackers (pee, poo, feeding, sleep) are great!"
- Always know which side baby nursed on last
- Helpful for "new parent brain fog" to avoid overfeeding - called "a life saver"
- Easy to track wet diapers when losing count

### Community & Support
- Anonymous posting makes asking for help easier
- "Helps acknowledge you're not alone on this journey"
- Partner sharing allows caregivers to stay in sync

### Design & Customization
- "Much prettier interface" compared to some alternatives
- Fun baby size comparisons (Parisian bakery, weird-but-cute animals, fun & games themes)
- Users like visual elements like "the hand thing" showing baby growth

### Continuity
- Natural progression for users who used Ovia Fertility and Ovia Pregnancy apps
- Provides continued support after pregnancy app is no longer needed

## User Cons (Real User Feedback)

### Technical Issues
- **Feeding tracker is glitchy:** "If you open the feeding tracker it gets stuck on the time from when you open it, so you can't enter a later time unless you save or cancel and reopen"
- **Midnight confusion:** App gets confused when entering data around midnight
- **App is slow to load** according to some users
- Partner/spouse won't use it due to glitchiness - "we stopped leveraging it after about 2 weeks because it was annoying"

### Missing Features
- **No weight/height tracking:** "I wish there was a way to enter baby's weight/height to see how much they grow"
- **No pump tracker:** Cannot log breast milk pumping sessions
- **No mood tracker:** Despite asking about parent mental health and Edinburgh scale, no mood tracking for postpartum depression monitoring
- **No analytics/trends:** "There's all this data but no easy way to compare day to day" - want average wet diapers, average length of feeds, anticipated nap windows
- **Limited data logging:** Cannot log notes, temperatures, or medications
- **No vaccination/immunization schedule tracking**
- **No doctor appointment tracking**
- **Limited measurement options:** Need options beyond ounces (like ml)
- **No feeding alarms:** Cannot set notifications for when baby has gone too long without eating

### Design Complaints
- UI update changed background to white, making data entry form harder to navigate
- Calendar functionality differs from other Ovia apps
- Some users find the charting/data visualization "busy and confusing"

### Compared to Alternatives
- Other apps (like Huckleberry) show feeding timer on home screen
- FertilityFriend provides better data analysis according to some users
- Some competitors offer better feeding/sleep pattern visualization

## Privacy Concerns

### Data Sharing with Employers
- **Washington Post (2019):** Reported that Ovia shares aggregated, de-identified user data with employers who pay for the service
- **Employer Data Access:** Can view employees' aggregated data including:
  - Average age and number of children
  - Current trimester
  - Average time to conceive
  - Percentage with high-risk pregnancies
  - C-section and premature birth rates
  - Return-to-work timing

### Re-identification Risk
- Experts warn that despite de-identification, companies may still identify which data belongs to which employee, especially in smaller workplaces with few pregnant employees

### Location Data Concerns (Post-Roe)
- App requires users to input country and state of residence
- In mid-2024, social media users (including Reddit with 14,000+ upvotes on WitchesVsPatriarchy) raised concerns about this data being subpoenaed in abortion-related cases
- Ovia states location is collected to comply with locality privacy laws

### Facebook Data Collection
- Facebook technology used for login allows Facebook to collect device information and engagement data
- Data used for personalized advertising on and off Facebook
- Collection occurs whether or not you use Facebook login feature

### Mozilla Privacy Rating
- Mozilla's "Privacy Not Included" review flagged the app
- JustUseApp Safety Score: 35.2/100 (appears generally safe but "use with caution")

### Ovia's Privacy Claims
- Claims health data is not shared with employers unless you "expressly opt-in"
- However, may share data with employer health plans and business associates consistent with HIPAA
- Claims sexual activity, MyQ question answers, and Community submissions are not shared
