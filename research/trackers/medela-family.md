# Medela Family (MyMedela)

## Overview
- **Developer:** Medela AG
- **Price:** Free
- **App Store Rating:** 4.6/5
- **Platform:** iOS, Android, WatchOS, Wear OS

---

## Pricing

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| Core App | FREE |
| VIP Pack | FREE (requires compatible Medela Bluetooth pump) |
| 24/7 LC Service | Optional subscription (USA only, specific pricing not disclosed) |

**Important:** Premium features are locked behind hardware purchase, not software subscription. VIP features effectively cost $200-$500+ (the price of a compatible pump). Users with rented pumps (like Medela Symphony) cannot access VIP features.

---

## Free Features

### Baby and Pregnancy Tracking
- Pregnancy tracking with contraction monitoring
- Baby size visualization during pregnancy
- Sleep tracking
- Diaper change logging
- Weight and length tracking
- Bottle feeding tracking
- Support for tracking multiple babies

### Breastfeeding Tracking
- Breastfeeding session timer
- Left/right breast tracking
- Daily and weekly volume statistics
- Manual data entry for pumping sessions
- Session history and logs

### Educational Content
- Expert tips and articles from certified lactation consultants
- Personal helper that answers questions using Medela's knowledge base
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding guidance

### Organization Tools
- Pre-set and customizable checklists (hospital bag, return to work, etc.)
- Dashboard with statistics and insights
- Export records to share with pediatrician
- Night mode for easier reading in dim lighting

---

## Paid Features (VIP Pack)

The VIP Pack is NOT available through monetary purchase. It is unlocked ONLY by connecting a compatible Medela Bluetooth breast pump (Sonata, Freestyle Flex, or Swing Maxi).

### VIP Pack Features Include:
- Real-time automatic pumping session recording via Bluetooth
- **Virtual Milk Storage:** Smart milk inventory system with fridge/freezer tracking and expiration alerts
- **Voice Control:** Voice-activated session tracking for hands-free logging
- **Caregiver Access:** Invite caregivers (partner, nanny, grandparents) to view and create trackings
- **Smart Watch App:** Available for WatchOS and Wear OS with full tracking capabilities

## User Opinions (from Reddit & Forums)

### Pros
- **Intuitive Design:** "Very user friendly, easy to navigate" with a bright, cheerful interface
- **Comprehensive Tracking:** "A lifesaver for tracking when we last fed/diapered our little one"
- **Smart Pump Integration:** Automatic session recording when Bluetooth works properly
- **Award-Winning:** Best Baby App of 2022 (Baby Innovation Awards)
- **Educational Content:** Expert tips from certified lactation consultants
- **Milk Stash Feature:** Virtual storage helps manage inventory and prevent milk waste
- **Night Mode:** Appreciated for late-night feedings
- **Multiple Baby Support:** Can track multiple children

### Cons

**Bluetooth Connectivity (Major Issue):**
- Frequent disconnections between pump and phone
- "Doesn't sync well with Sonata pump - it would take days to sync"
- "CALL" error requires contacting Medela for reset

**App Stability:**
- Freezing and crashing issues persist (reported as recently as December 2025)
- Requires reinstalling app to fix issues
- Buttons sometimes "stick" or register multiple times

**Data Syncing Problems:**
- Struggles with two people logged into the same account
- Data getting mixed up with incorrect dates
- Server connectivity errors even with working internet

**User Interface Complaints:**
- Logging past sessions is "nearly impossible"
- Small touch targets cause input errors
- Timer persists in notification even after session ends

**Feature Limitations:**
- VIP features only for specific Medela pump owners
- Users with rented Symphony pumps excluded from milk storage feature
- Requires internet connection for pump session recording

**Customer Support:**
- "Very poor" response times
- In-app feedback feature reportedly broken
