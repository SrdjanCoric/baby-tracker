# Top 15 iOS Baby Tracker Apps by Popularity

A curated list of the most popular iOS applications for tracking baby care activities, ranked by App Store ratings, downloads, and market presence.

---

## 1. What to Expect (Pregnancy Baby Tracker)
- **Developer:** Everyday Health
- **Price:** Free
- **App Store Rating:** 4.9/5 (300,000+ ratings)
- **Features:** Baby growth tracking, month-by-month milestones, feeding, diapers, sleep logging
- **Notable:** Called the pregnancy "bible"; trusted by 15+ million parents; featured in Women's Health; doctor recommended

---

## 2. BabyCenter Pregnancy & Baby Tracker
- **Developer:** BabyCenter LLC
- **Price:** Free with in-app purchases
- **App Store Rating:** 4.89/5 (270,000 ratings)

- **Notable:** 10+ million downloads; Apple featured app; Birth Club community; available since 2010

## 3. Baby Tracker - Newborn Log
